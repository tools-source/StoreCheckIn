StoreCheckIn Support

Last updated: March 10, 2026

StoreCheckIn is an employee time clock app. If you need help with sign in, subscriptions, reminders, or data behavior, contact support and include your app version and a short description of the issue.

Common Help Topics

  • Restore subscription purchases
  • Manage or cancel an active subscription
  • Employee records, check-in/check-out behavior, and timesheet totals
  • Shift reminder notifications

Subscription Management

Subscriptions are billed and managed by Apple. Manage or cancel your subscription at: apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions

Refund requests are handled by Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com
