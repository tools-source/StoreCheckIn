StoreCheckIn Support Effective date: March 10, 2026 This support page explains how to get help with StoreCheckIn. Overview StoreCheckIn is an employee time clock app that uses Sign in with Apple and App Store subscriptions. If you need help, contact support with your app version, device model, and a short description of the issue. Contact Email support: majdinagi@gmail.com Common Help Topics You can contact support for help with: Sign in with Apple account access issues Restoring purchases Managing or canceling an active subscription Employee records, check-in/check-out behavior, and timesheet totals Shift reminder notifications Subscription Management Subscriptions are billed and managed by Apple. Manage or cancel your subscription: https://apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions Request a refund through Apple: https://reportaproblem.apple.com/ Policies Privacy Policy: https://github.com/tools-source/StoreCheckIn/blob/feature/StoreCheckin/docs/privacy.html Terms of Use (EULA): https://www.apple.com/legal/internet-services/itunes/dev/stdeula/
