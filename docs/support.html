StoreCheckIn Support Effective date: March 13, 2026 This support page explains how to get help with StoreCheckIn. Overview StoreCheckIn is an employee time clock app that currently supports Sign in with Apple, App Store subscriptions, employee check-in and check-out tracking, archive history, weekly archive filtering inside archived employee records, shift reminder notifications, and app lock protection with PIN and biometrics. If you need help, contact support with your app version, device model, and a short description of the issue. Contact Email support: majdinagi@gmail.com Common Help Topics You can contact support for help with: Sign in with Apple account access issues Forgotten PIN reset and Apple account re-authentication Face ID or Touch ID unlock issues Restoring purchases Managing or canceling an active subscription Employee records, check-in/check-out behavior, and timesheet totals Archive/history behavior, including weekly archive filtering Shift reminder notifications for check-in and check-out Store name settings on the Employees tab Delete Account and removing local app data from the device Subscription Management Subscriptions are billed and managed by Apple. Manage or cancel your subscription: https://apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions Request a refund through Apple: https://reportaproblem.apple.com/ Policies Privacy Policy: https://tools-source.github.io/StoreCheckIn/privacy.html Terms of Use (EULA): https://www.apple.com/legal/internet-services/itunes/dev/stdeula/
