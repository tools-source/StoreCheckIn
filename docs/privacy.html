StoreCheckIn Privacy Policy Effective date: March 3, 2026 This Privacy Policy explains what information StoreCheckIn uses, how that information is handled, and what choices you have when using the app. Overview StoreCheckIn is an employee time clock app that uses Sign in with Apple, App Store subscriptions, local notifications, and Apple cloud services to provide sign-in, access control, data storage, and reminder features. Information We Collect We may use the following categories of information: Account information, including your Apple user identifier and, if provided through Sign in with Apple, your name and email address App content, including employee names, roles, hourly rates, notes, check-in and check-out records, timesheet data, and shift schedule settings that you enter in the app Subscription status information provided by Apple through the App Store Notification permission status on your device when you enable shift reminders How We Use Information We use information to: authenticate you with Sign in with Apple display and manage employee records, timesheets, and shift schedules determine whether your account has an active subscription required to use the app schedule local reminder notifications for employee shift start and end times when you enable that feature keep your data associated with the Apple account used to access the app Storage and Sync StoreCheckIn uses Apple technologies, including iCloud and CloudKit where available, to store and sync app data connected to your Apple account. Employee and timesheet records are intended to remain associated with the Apple account used in the app. If iCloud or CloudKit is unavailable, app data may be stored locally on the device until syncing becomes available again or local storage is used as a fallback. Subscriptions StoreCheckIn uses App Store subscriptions for access to paid features. Subscription billing, renewal, cancellation, and payment processing are handled by Apple. StoreCheckIn does not collect or store your payment card information. Notifications If you enable shift reminders for an employee, StoreCheckIn schedules local notifications on your device at the configured shift start and shift end times. These reminders are generated on-device and can be disabled at any time in iOS Settings. Sharing StoreCheckIn does not sell your personal information. Information is shared only with Apple services required for app functionality, including authentication, subscription handling, cloud storage, and syncing. Retention and Control You can: sign out of the app at any time edit or delete employee records inside the app manage your subscription through your Apple account subscription settings turn off notification permissions in iOS Settings remove app data by deleting records in the app and, where applicable, deleting the app or managing iCloud data through Apple services Contact If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or data handling in StoreCheckIn, contact: majdinagi@gmail.com
