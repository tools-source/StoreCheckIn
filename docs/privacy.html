StoreCheckIn Privacy Policy Effective date: March 13, 2026 This Privacy Policy explains what information StoreCheckIn uses, how that information is handled, and what choices you have when using the app. Overview StoreCheckIn is an employee time clock app for store teams. The current app includes Sign in with Apple, App Store subscriptions, employee check-in and check-out tracking, archive/history tools, local reminder notifications, a store name setting, and app lock protection with a 6-digit PIN plus Face ID or Touch ID when available. Information We Collect We may use the following categories of information: Account information, including your Apple user identifier and, if provided through Sign in with Apple, your name and email address App content, including store name, employee names, roles, hourly rates, notes, shift schedules, active time entries, archived time entries, and timesheet data that you enter in the app Subscription status information provided by Apple through the App Store Notification permission status on your device when you enable employee shift reminders Security settings stored on your device, including whether app lock is enabled, whether biometric unlock is enabled, and a hashed form of your 6-digit PIN How We Use Information We use information to: authenticate you with Sign in with Apple display and manage employee records, timesheets, archive history, weekly archive filtering, and store settings determine whether your account has an active subscription required to use the app schedule local reminder notifications for employee shift start and shift end times when you enable that feature protect access to the app with a PIN and biometric unlock when enabled support PIN reset after Apple account re-authentication keep employee data associated with the Apple account used in the app Storage and Sync StoreCheckIn uses Apple technologies, including CloudKit where available, to store and sync employee and time clock data connected to your Apple account. If CloudKit is unavailable, app data may be stored locally on the device as a fallback. App lock settings such as the hashed PIN and biometric unlock preference are stored locally on the device. The store name setting is also stored locally for the signed-in account on that device. Subscriptions StoreCheckIn uses App Store subscriptions for access to paid features. Subscription billing, renewal, cancellation, refunds, and payment processing are handled by Apple. StoreCheckIn does not collect or store your payment card information. Notifications If you enable shift reminders for an employee, StoreCheckIn schedules local notifications on your device at the configured shift start and shift end times. These reminders are generated on-device and can be disabled at any time in iOS Settings. Sharing StoreCheckIn does not sell your personal information. Information is shared only with Apple services required for app functionality, including authentication, subscription handling, CloudKit storage and syncing, and device notification delivery. Retention and Control You can: sign out of the app at any time edit, archive, clear, or delete employee records and time entries inside the app set or remove a store name in Settings enable, change, or disable the app PIN and biometric unlock options reset a forgotten PIN by re-authenticating with the same Apple account used in the app manage your subscription through your Apple account subscription settings turn off notification permissions in iOS Settings use Delete Account in Settings to remove this account's local app data, reminders, and store settings from the current device remove the app and, where applicable, manage CloudKit or iCloud data through Apple services Contact If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or data handling in StoreCheckIn, contact: majdinagi@gmail.com
